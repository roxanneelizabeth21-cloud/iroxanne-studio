Additional revision rounds are billed at sixty-five dollars ($65) per hour.

(c) If the amounts already paid exceed the value of completed work, the Studio refunds the excess, except that the deposit is non-refundable once work has begun.
(d) If the Client cancels before any work has begun, the Studio refunds the deposit less two hundred fifty dollars ($250) for the reserved build slot.

(f) The license in Section 10 terminates on cancellation. Upon payment of any balance owed under (b), the Studio will deliver the work completed to date AS IS, without warranty and without obligation to complete it, and ownership of that partial work transfers under Section 10.
